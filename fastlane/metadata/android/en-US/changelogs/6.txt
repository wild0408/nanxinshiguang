Nanxin Shiguang 1.0.5 update.

- New app icon: teal-to-blue gradient with a white timetable card and orbiting time trail, plus a themed icon (monochrome) layer for Android 13+.

- Added version-comparison and update-endpoint tests (43 -> 47).
